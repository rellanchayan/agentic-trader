## Lessons for Tomorrow

1. **One tick is not a running loop.** A single entry in the intraday log at any time
   of day does not mean the scheduler is healthy. The diagnostic question is: did ticks
   fire at 9:30, 9:32, 9:34, 9:36... continuously? If not, the loop is broken regardless
   of whether one late evaluation occurred.

2. **The NVDA reversal exposed a gap in risk-on thesis tracking.** Today's plan was
   built on NDX +1.63% and oil -6.7%. By 10:40 AM, NVDA was below its ORB low at
   $199.155. A running tick loop would have seen this reversal develop in real time and
   would have had accurate context about whether the risk-on regime was still intact.
   Without that coverage, all we know is the snapshot at 10:40 AM — not what happened
   in between.

3. **Seven sessions without a trade means the system has not been tested since June 18.**
   The strategy logic (ORB evaluation, VWAP conditions, volume gates) was executed
   correctly at 10:40 AM today. The tick logic works. The scheduler does not. Those
   are two separate problems. The strategy logic being sound is not the bottleneck;
   getting it to run 74 times per session, starting at 9:30 AM, is.
