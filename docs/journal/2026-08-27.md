## What Didn't Work

**Fourteen consecutive sessions without a realized P&L, including today — a day the plan
explicitly classified as GO.**

This is the core problem and it must be stated directly. The prior thirteen zero-trade sessions
had varying degrees of plan-level justification: MIXED/RANGE tapes, active macro events,
earnings overhangs, known spread blowouts across the watchlist. Some of those zero-trade
outcomes were clearly correct.

Today is different. The plan said RISK-ON. It armed QQQ and SPY unconditionally. It noted that
QQQ and SPY have no spread concerns and no earnings binary risk. These are the two most liquid
ETFs in US markets. The plan's last paragraph named the 13-session streak directly and warned
against self-imposing a zero-trade outcome on a day that genuinely qualifies.

The result was still zero trades.

There are three interpretations, all of which must be considered honestly:

1. **The gates correctly blocked entry every tick.** If NQ futures' +0.9% gain was absorbed in
   the premarket and QQQ's ORB never produced a clean directional break with rel vol >1.5x,
   then zero trades is the right answer. Tape conditions at the actual open may not have matched
   the premarket conditions that informed the plan.

2. **The 1.5x rel-vol gate is set too high for the current market regime.** If QQQ regularly
   produces rel vol in the 1.2-1.4x range on most days and the 1.5x threshold almost never
   clears, the gate is miscalibrated. This would explain the streak without any behavioral issue
   — the setup just cannot trigger under current calibration.

3. **Hesitation or structural passivity prevented execution.** If the setup was present and the
   gates passed, but no order was submitted, this is a bot behavior problem. No data in the
   summary JSON can confirm or deny this.

The streak is long enough and the plan-vs-outcome gap today is wide enough that this must be
escalated for review — not of individual gates on individual days, but of whether the system as
a whole is capable of producing orders at the frequency the strategy requires. **A trading
system that produces zero orders on a GO day with two unconditional armed names is either
calibrated too conservatively or is not functioning as designed. We do not know which. That is
the problem.**

**No tick-level logging means this question cannot be answered from available data.** The
absence of order logs when zero orders are placed is the most significant operational gap in
the current system. It is the second session in a row where this limitation has been noted.
