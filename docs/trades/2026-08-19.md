- Relative volume above 2.0x (elevated gate for MIXED/RANGE regime; standard is 1.5x)
- Live spread below 5 bp

**10:39 AM evaluation — why no trade:**

SPY was trading at $771.11, confirmed above the ORB high of $770.59. The breakout signal was
real. The spread was not the blocking factor. The single disqualifying condition: relative
volume was 1.72x — below the plan's required 2.0x threshold for ORB breakout entries on
MIXED/RANGE tape.

mid-session macro event tend to produce a burst of opening volume that fades quickly, and a
breakout on declining volume in front of a 2:00 PM binary event carries a materially higher
failure probability than a breakout with confirmed institutional participation. At 1.72x, the
plan judged participation insufficient. In hindsight: SPY was at $769.22 by 15:40 PM ($1.87
below the ORB high), meaning the breakout did not hold through the session. The gate was
correct.

**9:39 AM evaluation — why no trade:**

gate. The entry window had not yet opened (9:45 AM start). Three simultaneous disqualifying

**Outcome:** No trade. $0 P&L. This was the correct outcome.
