  correctly mapped the macro risk (quarter-end, 10:00 AM economic prints) to a cautious MIXED
  regime. This is the third consecutive session with a premarket plan, continuing an improving
  habit.

- The plan contained an explicit no-trade trigger ("no clean setup by 11:30 AM = no-trade day")
  and that trigger fired correctly. The plan was not ignored — the intraday log at 12:39 PM
  directly cites it.

- An intraday log was written today. docs/intraday/2026-06-30.md exists. After two consecutive
  sessions (2026-06-26, 2026-06-29) with no intraday log at all, writing one — even a single

  relative volumes, NVDA's price vs. VWAP vs. ORB high, and the explicit rule being applied (ORB

  but had not cleared the ORB high by $1.24. Trading a name that is "close but not there" is
  exactly the kind of marginal entry the playbook warns against, especially on a MIXED regime day.
  No trade is the right call when the signal hasn't triggered.

- The AMZN elevated-bar rule (from the -$64.44 loss on 2026-06-24) remained in force and is
  reflected in the plan's priority ranking. Discipline on losing names is being maintained.

- The account ended flat. Zero positions confirmed. No overnight exposure heading into July.

**What didn't work:**

  Between 9:30 AM and 12:39 PM there were roughly 95 two-minute ticks. We have no record of any of

  instead of zero). The root cause — whether the loop ran and did not log, or did not run — is
  still undiagnosed.

- NVDA was $1.24 from an ORB breakout signal on a day when it had the best volume (1.31x) and was
  above VWAP. Whether it ever threatened the $199.04 level before or after 12:39 PM is unknown,
  because we only have one snapshot. If it did break $199.04 before the ORB window closed at
  11:15 AM and the tick loop wasn't running, we missed a signal. If it never got there, the
  no-trade call was entirely correct. We cannot distinguish these two outcomes with one log entry.

- The broader win-rate picture remains poor: 22.22% (2 wins in 9 trades), avg_win_r 0.60,

## Lessons for Tomorrow

1. The tick loop's logging behavior is still unverified. Three sessions now show either no intraday
   log (2026-06-26, 2026-06-29) or one entry late in the day (today). Before the next session,
   confirm whether the loop is actually firing at 2-minute intervals and whether its logging path
   is writing to docs/intraday/. A loop that runs silently and produces one entry at noon is as
   opaque as a loop that doesn't run.

2. One intraday snapshot is not enough to evaluate the session honestly. Even on a no-trade day,
   every tick should produce at least a one-line entry in the intraday log: what each screened name
   was doing, whether it was approaching a level, and why no action was taken. The 12:39 PM entry
   today is the right format — it just needs to exist at every tick, not only once.

3. The win rate (22.22%) and inverted reward/risk (avg_win 0.60R vs. avg_loss -1.46R) mean that
   when entries are taken, the selection or execution is off. With only 9 historical trades this
   is a small sample, but the direction is clear. The tuner is unfrozen today — the next clean
   round-trip (win or loss) should be examined carefully against entry criteria before any
   parameter is adjusted.
