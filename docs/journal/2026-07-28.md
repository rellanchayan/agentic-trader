## Lessons for Tomorrow

1. **The tick at 9:31 AM is before the ORB window — the loop must sustain past the
   open.** A tick firing at the open is evidence the scheduler is alive at 9:31 AM; it is not
   evidence it stays alive through 9:45, 9:47, 9:49... The critical window is 9:45 AM to
   11:00 AM. That is where trades happen. A loop that fires once at 9:31 and goes dark has
   missed the entire productive window.

2. **NFLX's spread at 9:31 AM was 47 bp, not a reason to stop the loop.** A spread gate
   failure at the open should generate a "spread too wide, will re-check at next tick" log
   entry and keep running. It should not be the last entry of the session. If the loop is
   dying after the first condition failure, that is a logic error in the loop's exit handling,
   not a market conditions problem.

3. **Eight no-trade sessions in a row means the strategy has not been tested since June 18.**
   The tuner shows 11 lifetime trades on 26 days of history — 0.42 trades per day on average.
   That average is dragged entirely by the first few weeks. The last 18 trading days have
   produced zero. No amount of journal writing or plan refinement matters until the tick loop
   runs continuously for a full session. That is the only fix that counts.
