## Lessons for Tomorrow, 2026-09-02

1. **A VWAP reclaim in a heavy macro risk-off environment has a higher-than-normal failure rate;
   require stronger confirmation before entry.** Today's entry conditions (1.87x rel vol, 0.6 bp
   spread, price $2.05 above VWAP at entry) were technically sufficient. The problem was the
   macro backdrop — surging yields, oil above $92, geopolitical tail risk — which produced a
   dead-cat bounce rather than a sustained reclaim. In future risk-off sessions with multiple
   stacked macro headwinds, consider requiring a longer candle hold above VWAP (two confirmed
   ticks, not one) before submitting the buy order. One tick of confirmation is not enough when
   the macro tide is running against the trade.

2. **The win rate of 33.3% and average loss of -0.834R are not compatible with a positive-expectancy
   system; the edge question must be resolved before increasing activity.** Expectancy = (win rate
   x avg win) - (loss rate x avg loss) = (0.333 x 0.38R) - (0.667 x 0.834R) = 0.127R - 0.556R =
   -0.429R per trade. The system is currently edge-negative. More trades at this expectancy deepens
   the loss. The tuner is correctly frozen. The priority is to identify which setup(s) produce
   positive outcomes (the ORB breakout on NFLX on August 7 at +0.345R was a win; the VWAP reclaim
   today at -0.673R was a loss) and understand the conditions under which each setup is and is not
   valid before widening deployment.

3. **The thesis-break exit rule is worth preserving and extending.** Exiting at $708.234 instead
   of $707.30 saved $9.34 on a $19.22 loss — a 33% reduction in loss magnitude. The rule is: if
   the specific condition that justified the entry (VWAP reclaim: price above VWAP) has been
   continuously false for 60+ minutes, exit without waiting for the hard stop. This rule is working.
   Consider formalizing it in the playbook: "For any VWAP reclaim trade, if price falls and remains
   below VWAP continuously for two or more tick cycles (>= 4 minutes), exit. Do not wait for the
   hard stop unless price is within $0.30 of it."
