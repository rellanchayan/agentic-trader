## What Didn't Work

**The SPY setup was structurally narrow from the start.** The VWAP reclaim thesis required
SPY to find a low, stabilize, and then reclaim VWAP from below — a classic risk-off mean
reversion. Instead, SPY opened partially above VWAP and then drifted lower through the day.
The setup never materialized inside the window because the market did not give the flush-and-stabilize
pattern that the thesis needed. This is not a failure of execution; it is a mismatch between
the planned setup structure and what the tape actually delivered. Noted for calibration.

**The watchlist is thin.** Four names screened; three disarmed before the open; one conditional.
When the sole conditional setup fails to trigger, the session produces zero trades by design.
The playbook calls for a substitute non-semiconductor name when the sector disarm has been
active for two or more consecutive sessions (which triggered SPY today), but a single substitute
is not enough redundancy on days with heavy macro suppression. Future pre-FOMC-minutes Tuesdays
should include a second qualified substitute in the plan.

## Lessons for Tomorrow

1. **On pre-FOMC Tuesdays, a zero-trade plan is a real plan.** Tomorrow (Wednesday) has the
   FOMC July minutes at 2:00 PM ET. That event is tomorrow; today's suppression was the
   pre-positioning. Do not mistake a correct zero-trade day for a calibration problem. If the
   setup does not appear, the playbook says so and that is the trade.

2. **A reclaim before the entry window opens does not count.** At 9:40 AM, SPY was already above
   VWAP before the 9:45 AM gate opened. A VWAP reclaim that happens in the first ten minutes of
   the session — before the ORB has formed and before the entry window is open — is noise, not
   signal. The setup requires a fresh reclaim inside the window. This should be made explicit in
   the playbook entry for the VWAP reclaim setup so it is unambiguous on future similar days.

3. **Two armed setups are better than one on risk-off days.** When the semiconductor sector is
   disarmed and the playbook calls for a substitute, consider arming two substitutes (e.g.,
   SPY and QQQ, or SPY and IWM) rather than one. If the single substitute's conditional setup
   fails to trigger, the session produces zero trades by construction. Two conditional setups
   double the chance that at least one clean entry materializes, without violating any hard limit.
