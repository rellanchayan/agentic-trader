   lesson from every journal since mid-September. It is the first, second, and third priority.

   is the minimum viable process action — it maintains market context, engages the
   acknowledgment mechanism, and preserves the analytical record. Skipping it today was a
   regression with no upside.

   orb at -0.20R, momentum at -0.98R, and vwap_reclaim at -0.37R mean that even a fully
   functional session with ten trades and an 81% fill rate would be expected to lose money.
   Fixing the scheduler is necessary but not sufficient. The strategy parameters — stop placement,
   entry criteria, R-targets — need review against the win-rate and R-ratio data before trading
   resumes at volume.
