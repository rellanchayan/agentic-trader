## Setups Armed — Mixed / Range Regime

In a mixed regime, the following setup hierarchy applies:

**Armed (use these):**
1. VWAP reclaim — price drops below VWAP after the open, then reclaims it with increasing
   volume. Enter on the first candle that closes back above VWAP. Stop just below VWAP.
   Target 1.5x the stop distance. This is the primary setup today.
2. ORB breakout — ONLY after the opening range (first 15 minutes, 9:30-9:45 AM) has fully
   formed. ONLY with volume confirmation (relative volume > 1.2x). ONLY in names gapping or
   showing clear strength (INTC is the main ORB candidate today). No ORB trades on NFLX or
   PFE without a strong specific catalyst.
3. Tight mean-reversion on low-ATR names (BAC, PFE) — small size, tight stop, only in a
   clearly range-bound session (SPY within 0.5 ATR of open price).

**Disarmed (do not use today):**
- Momentum chasing / adding to a winner after it has already moved 1+ ATR from the open.
- Buying breakouts in names that are gapping down (NFLX, AMZN) — gap-down names under
  pressure in a mixed market typically fail breakout attempts.
- Any trade in the first 5 minutes of the session (9:30-9:34 AM). Let the spread normalize.
- New entries after 3:30 PM ET. Start closing by 3:50 PM. Be flat by 3:55 PM.

**Size guidance for today:** Use half to two-thirds of the $10K per-trade limit given the
mixed regime. On a day where the index is flat and geopolitical noise is alive, smaller size
