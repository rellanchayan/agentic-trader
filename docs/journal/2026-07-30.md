## Lessons for Tomorrow

1. **AMZN-like days — armed primary candidate, clear thesis, favorable regime — cannot produce
   zero trades without an explanation.** Tomorrow, if the plan arms a primary candidate, the
   tick loop must either submit an order or write a specific, timestamped reason why the gate
   failed at the moment of evaluation. "Zero trades" without a tick record is not a documented
   decision; it is an undocumented gap.

2. **The $16.65 equity gain is paper-account interest, not trading income. Track it separately.**
   The daily summary's realized_pnl field ($0.00) is the correct number for trading performance.
   The equity-level change includes non-trading adjustments. Conflating the two would misstate
   the trading record.

3. **Twenty sessions without a trade is the system's defining problem right now — not the
   watchlist composition, not the spread thresholds, not the position sizing.** The loop
   cadence failure has turned a well-designed intraday system into a premarket-only system that
   writes plans it never executes. Every other improvement is irrelevant until the tick loop
   runs consistently at 2-minute cadence for a full session.
