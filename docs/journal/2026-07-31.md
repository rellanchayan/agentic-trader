## Lessons for Tomorrow

1. **A stop level in a JSON file is not a stop order.** Every buy submission must be followed
   immediately by a corresponding stop-loss order (or bracket order) in Alpaca. If the
   stop order submission fails, the buy must be cancelled or immediately flattened. "The stop
   is documented" is not the same as "the stop is in the market."

2. **A 2.5R loss on a 1R-risk trade is a process failure, not a market failure.** The setup,
   the entry, and the sizing were correct. The market moved against us — that happens. What
   should not happen is holding through 2.5x the intended risk because no mechanism existed to
   close the position when the thesis was invalidated. Separate the entry decision (which was
   sound) from the exit execution (which was absent).

3. **Month-end gap stocks deserve tighter stop placement or smaller size.** The plan correctly
   flagged month-end institutional rebalancing as a risk. A 12% earnings gap on the last Friday
   of the month combines volatile tape conditions with aggressive book-squaring. On these days,
   either reduce size by 50%, tighten the stop to the midpoint of the ORB range rather than
   below the ORB high, or set a harder time exit (e.g., close by 1:00 PM regardless). The
   current structure — full size, stop at ORB high, hold until flatten — is too loose for that
   combination of risk factors.
