## What Worked / What Did Not Work

**What worked:**

- **The mandatory 15-minute no-entry boundary after a data event.** At 09:41 INTC had the most
  attractive opening metrics of the three-day window: 5.08x relative volume, 3.9 bp spread, above
  VWAP, catalyst-driven gap. The timing gate blocked the entry. By 10:39, NVDA had failed the
  broad-tape unlock and INTC's relative volume was already declining (2.17x). The one tick with
  genuinely good INTC metrics was correctly unreachable.

- **The broad-tape unlock gate (SPY AND NVDA above VWAP).** NVDA was below VWAP at every
  evaluable tick today. The gate blocked correctly on all six ticks where an entry would have been
  theoretically possible. This is five consecutive sessions where NVDA's VWAP position has been the
  primary controlling variable.

- **The elevated rel-vol threshold (2.0x) for MIXED/RANGE gap-up days.** The standard gate is 1.5x.
  By 10:39 INTC was at 2.17x — marginally above the standard gate but still above the elevated one.
  By 11:40 it had fallen to 1.54x, below both thresholds. The elevated standard added a meaningful
  buffer on a day where the tape faded immediately after the open.

- **The 90-minute ORB window.** At 11:40 the ORB window had expired (130 minutes since open). This
  was the third independent blocking condition that tick, alongside the NVDA VWAP failure and the
  VWAP reclaim prohibition. Having a hard time-based window prevents late-chase entries into a
  fading setup.

- **No drift into the afternoon session.** By 13:39 NVDA was 0.56x relative volume and had widened
  its VWAP gap from -$0.70 at 11:40 to -$1.17. The tape was deteriorating, not improving. The log
  called this out cleanly: "NVDA deteriorating — further below VWAP than any prior tick today."
  No attempt to find a late-session entry on a dead tape.

**What did not work:**

- **The Alpaca outage at 12:39 UTC.** One tick was missed because the preflight gate returned EXIT
  (Alpaca unreachable). No positions were open so no operational harm resulted, but this is a gap
  in the intraday record. If the tick had identified an exit signal for an open position, the miss
  would have mattered. The outage is documented; whether it is a recurring pattern requires checking
  against prior tick logs.

- **INTC as a watchlist name on a pre-FOMC week session.** INTC was the most individually
  interesting name for the third session in a row, but the semiconductor sector disarm has been
  active for three consecutive sessions. A name that keeps generating "interesting but blocked"

  practice; whether INTC should remain on the watchlist at all through a sector disarm is worth
