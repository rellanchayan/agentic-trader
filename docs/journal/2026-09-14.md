## What Worked / What Did Not Work

**What worked:**

- **The broad-tape unlock gate.** At the one tick where QQQ individually looked tradeable
  (10:39 ET — 2.10x relative volume, 3.0 bp spread, confirmed ORB break), NVDA was 6 cents
  below VWAP and SPY was $1.06 below VWAP. The broad-tape gate blocked the entry. NVDA did
  recover above VWAP at 11:40, but by then QQQ's relative volume had collapsed from 2.10x to
  1.55x and the 90-minute ORB window had expired. The gate system worked exactly as designed:
  the best-looking individual setup was blocked by a deteriorating broad tape.

- **The 90-minute ORB window.** The ORB window expired at approximately 11:00 AM ET (90 minutes
  after the opening range formed at 9:30 AM). After that time, QQQ could not be entered on an
  ORB thesis regardless of other conditions. This prevented any late-session chase into a
  compressing tape.

- **Vol compression confirmation as a tracking signal.** The intraday log recorded QQQ relative
  volume at every tick: 2.10x (10:39) → 1.55x (11:40) → 1.33x (12:40) → 1.13x (13:40) →
  1.04x (14:39) → 0.95x (15:40). This is a textbook pre-FOMC vol-compression decay curve. The
  session compressed steadily from open to close. Tracking this at each tick confirmed the
  regime diagnosis in the plan was correct and eliminated any temptation to chase a late-session
  entry.

- **The elevated 2.0x relative-volume gate for RISK-OFF + pre-FOMC sessions.** The standard
  gate is 1.5x. At the 2.0x elevated threshold, only the 10:39 tick was anywhere near a valid
  entry on volume alone — and at that tick the broad-tape unlock blocked it. By 11:40 QQQ was
  already below the standard 1.5x gate. The elevated threshold correctly prevented any entry.

- **The VWAP reclaim prohibition.** VWAP reclaim was off-table per the plan (RISK-OFF + imminent
  binary macro event). At no tick was there any temptation to fall back on VWAP reclaim to
  manufacture a trade. The prohibition held cleanly.

**What did not work:**

- **The plan path mismatch.** The premarket phase writes to `docs/plan/2026-09-14.md`; the tick
  process reads from `docs/plans/2026-09-14.md`. These are two different paths. The 09:39 tick
  logged a HALT because the plan was "missing" from the path it was checking. The plan was not
  actually missing — it was in the wrong directory. This is a systemic bug. It caused a spurious
  HALT log at 09:39 and means the session operated without the tick process being able to verify
  the plan. The fact that today's outcome was a correct zero-trade day does not excuse the bug:
  on a day where a position was open and needed to be verified against the plan-documented
  loss-stop baseline, this mismatch could cause material harm. The path must be reconciled before
  tomorrow.

- **INTC as a watchlist name under an extended sector disarm.** This is the fourth consecutive
  session where INTC has appeared on the watchlist and the fourth where the semiconductor sector
  disarm was active. INTC generated a -6.6% premarket gap on a sector-specific catalyst (AI
  safety essay + Apple dropping Intel Mac support) and was correctly flagged as WATCH ONLY all
  session. But a name that is WATCH ONLY for four consecutive sessions due to the same structural
  condition (sector disarm) should be evaluated for replacement. The plan noted this explicitly
  and recommended it. No action was taken today. This persists as an open item.
