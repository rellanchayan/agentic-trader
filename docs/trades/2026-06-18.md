## Card 2: MSFT ORB — LOSS

**What:** BUY 26 MSFT, limit $380.83 / SELL 26 MSFT, limit $377.27. LIMIT + DAY both legs.

**Entry fill:** $379.66 (significantly better than limit — $1.17 below)
**Exit fill:** $377.81 (better than exit limit of $377.27)
**Entry submitted:** ~1:36 PM ET
**Exit submitted:** ~2:35 PM ET

**Why we entered:** ORB breakout above the ORB high of $379.29. At entry, MSFT was above VWAP
($377.00), relative volume was 1.23x, quote freshness was 0.8 seconds, and spread was 7.9 basis
points. Stop was at $379.00, target at $384.49. The setup parameters all passed the entry gate.

**Why it was not the right call (in hindsight):** The entry time of 1:36 PM ET is the core problem.
ORB setups derive their edge from price resolving the opening range in the first 30 to 90 minutes
of session. By 1:36 PM, the "opening range" had been set roughly five hours earlier. A breakout at
that hour above a five-hour-old range high has much weaker structural support — the market has had
the full morning to absorb that level and has chosen not to break it cleanly until the afternoon.
Late ORB entries have lower continuation probability.

A secondary problem: the stop was at $379.00, and the fill was $379.66. The planned risk distance
was $0.66/share ($379.66 - $379.00). For MSFT, which routinely moves $2-3 in a single hour, a
$0.66 stop is inside the noise. It is easy for price to touch $378.90 intraday without the
breakout thesis being invalidated. The stop was so tight that any normal MSFT oscillation would
trigger it.

**Risk and stop:** Stop at $379.00. Risk from fill to stop: $379.66 - $379.00 = $0.66/share.
Total planned risk: 26 x $0.66 = $17.16.

**Exit rationale:** By 2:35 PM ET, MSFT had fallen to $377.86 — below the stop of $379.00 and
below the ORB high of $379.29. The breakout had failed. The stop-exit limit was submitted at
$377.27 and filled at $377.81, better than the limit. The stop rule was followed: once price is
below the stop and below the ORB high, exit without hesitation.

**R calculation note:** The exit loss of $1.85/share ($379.66 - $377.81) divided by the planned
risk of $0.66/share = -2.803R. This outsized R loss reflects a stop that was too tight for the
instrument at that time of day. The loss in dollars (-$48.10) is within the $10,000 single-trade
limit and well within the $3,000 daily stop, but -2.803R is a warning sign about stop placement
quality on this setup.

**Outcome:** -$48.10 gross P&L. -2.803R. LOSS.
