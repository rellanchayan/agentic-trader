The plan called for exactly this outcome: in a mixed / risk-off session, doing nothing is correct.

## What Worked / What Didn't

**What worked:**

- The premarket phase ran completely and correctly this time. The day plan is written, the watchlist
  is documented, the loss-stop baseline was captured at $999,932.42 at 9:29 AM UTC, and the opening
  levels file (levels.json) is fully populated with orb_high/orb_low values for all 5 names.
  This is the first session since 2026-06-18 with a complete premarket record.

- The system correctly identified the regime as risk-off within the first hour. SPY broke below its
  ORB low before the second tick and never recovered. The intraday log shows the bot recognized
  this explicitly at each tick and applied the correct response: VWAP reclaim only, with no ORB
  setups in a market trending lower.

- Every WAIT decision included a specific, verifiable reason citing exact prices, exact spread
  readings, and exact relative volume readings against hard config thresholds. This is exactly the
  level of decision documentation the playbook requires.

- INTC's spread filter correctly blocked the 9:33 AM entry (28.2bp vs 25bp max). This is the right
  call — if we had forced an entry 3bp outside the spread limit, we would have been buying a name
  in a fading market with a spread that was unusually wide, possibly a sign of bad liquidity at
  that moment.

- The account ended flat, confirmed. Zero overnight exposure. Loss-stop (day_stop.json active=false,
  reason="reset at open") never came close to triggering; P&L never moved from $0.

- The tick frequency was roughly hourly rather than every 2 minutes. The playbook notes that hourly
  checks are a "cloud mode" consideration requiring wider stops and smaller size. The session ran
  with that constraint acknowledged.

**What didn't work:**

- INTC, the day's lead candidate, ultimately closed above VWAP and above the premarket reference
  price of $139.05 (last tick had INTC at $140.86). Whether a trade opportunity existed and was
  missed due to a disqualifying condition, or whether the disqualifying conditions were genuinely
  correct barriers to entry on that specific day, is a fair question. The honest answer: the
  rel_volume checks (1.18, then 1.07) at the afternoon ticks were the blocking factor on an
  otherwise above-VWAP name. The min_rel_volume threshold of 1.20 is a config parameter. One
  should note that INTC's total volume today was 4.83M shares against a 10-day average of 5.1M —
  roughly 95% of average, not unusually low. The 1.20 relative volume gate may have been calibrated
  for a higher conviction threshold than what materialized today. This is worth watching.

- Ticks ran approximately once per hour rather than every 2 minutes as the architecture intends.
  The session covered 9:33 to 15:33 with only 7 ticks. In a 2-minute tick loop that would be ~195
  ticks. The coarser frequency means setups that appeared and resolved between ticks were invisible
  to the bot. Whether INTC offered a better entry earlier in the session — when its rel_vol was
  presumably higher and closer to the open — is unknown because the ticks were not dense enough to
  catch it.

- NFLX closed at $72.82, well below its premarket price of $76.55 and its ORB low of $75.63. The
  plan correctly rated NFLX as low-priority given its gap down and fundamental pressure. The outcome
  confirmed that call — NFLX fell further during the session. No regret there.

## Lessons for Tomorrow

1. The minimum relative volume threshold of 1.20 blocked INTC in the afternoon despite the name
   being above VWAP and above premarket. INTC's total volume today was ~95% of its 10-day average —
   not abnormally low. Consider whether 1.20 is the right threshold, or whether 1.10–1.15 would
   admit more legitimate setups without admitting junk. Any change should wait until the tuner is
   unlocked (5+ days of history) and should be made as a single logged step.

2. Tick frequency of once per hour instead of once per 2 minutes is a real gap. INTC moved from
   $137.72 at 10:33 to $140.86 at 15:33 — a $3.14 intraday range that the bot was never positioned
   to catch. The infrastructure running hourly ticks in a strategy designed for 2-minute ticks is
   the single biggest factor limiting the bot's ability to trade. Fix the tick frequency before
   analyzing why setups are not triggering.

3. Today's session proved that the system can correctly identify a risk-off day, avoid buying into
   weakness, and end flat. That is genuine progress. The next step is proving it can identify a
   risk-on moment within a mixed session and execute a single clean trade. The 2026-06-22 record
   is defensible — but a bot that only holds cash is not learning to trade.
