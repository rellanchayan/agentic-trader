## Lessons for Tomorrow

1. **The plan is not optional.** Writing `docs/plans/<date>.md` before the first tick is a hard
   rule, not a suggestion. Without it, there is no regime call, no setup specification, no entry
   gate, and no way to evaluate whether a zero-trade outcome was correct. Schedule the plan write
   as part of the premarket routine and confirm the file exists before the 9:30 AM open.

2. **A nine-session no-gain streak deserves a structured review, not another skipped day.**
   The correct response is not to lower gates or force trades — it is to run a deliberate audit:
   check whether the premarket screen is surfacing enough qualified setups, verify that the entry
   gates are calibrated for current conditions, and confirm the playbook still matches the regime.
   That review belongs in tomorrow's plan, not deferred to a later session.

3. **Zero trades with no plan is not the same as zero trades with a sound plan.** Both produce
   $0 P&L and a flat account, but only one produces a record that can teach something. The journal
   can document discipline on a no-trade day only if there was discipline to document. Today
   there was not — and that is the honest record.
