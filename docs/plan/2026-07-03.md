## Forward-Looking Regime for Monday July 6: MIXED

**One-sentence reason:** The Nasdaq fell 2%+ and AI/chip stocks dropped sharply on Thursday while
the Dow gained 1% — a clear sector rotation out of high-multiple growth into value — and entering
Monday with the AI overhang unresolved, the environment calls for selectivity and smaller size
rather than pressing momentum.

**The full picture:**

Thursday July 2 closed with a visible split. The Dow Jones rose 539 points (+1.03%), led by AAPL
(+4.8%), McDonald's (+4.1%), and Disney (+3.8%). At the same time, the Nasdaq 100 fell more than
2%, NVDA dropped approximately 6%, and INTC fell roughly 9%. The VanEck Semiconductor ETF (SMH)
shed 5% after a record 71% gain in Q2. This is not a broad risk-off day — it is a rotation:
money moved out of AI/chip names and into stocks with near-term earnings clarity or lower
multiples.

The triggering forces:
1. **AI demand skepticism**: OpenAI reportedly missed monthly user and revenue targets. SK Hynix
   announced plans to slow high-bandwidth memory (HBM) expansion. These two items together raise
   doubt about whether the AI capital spending cycle can sustain chip demand.
2. **Valuation pressure**: SMH was up 71% in Q2 alone. A 5% pullback on doubt is not a crash;
   it is a normal reset. The correction may not be finished.
3. **June NFP miss**: Nonfarm payrolls came in at 57,000 versus 110,000 expected. The unemployment
   rate was 4.2%. Prior months were revised down (April: -31K, May: -43K). The weak number
   removes near-term rate-hike risk — markets took a September hike off the table — but it also
   signals economic softening. For financials and rate-sensitive names this is modestly positive
   (flatter rate path). For growth/tech this is a two-edged sword.
4. **Fed chair Warsh**: Markets are watching for any hawkish remarks from the new Fed Chair, who
   has been more inflation-wary than his predecessor. FOMC minutes (from the April 28-29 meeting)
   are due Wednesday July 8 — a risk event for the week.

**Regime call:** MIXED. Defensive/value sectors (financials, consumer staples, large-cap quality)
look more stable. The AI/chip complex is under rotation pressure and trend is down for now. In
mixed conditions the playbook says: favor VWAP reclaims and mean-reversion over breakout momentum,
and trade smaller.
