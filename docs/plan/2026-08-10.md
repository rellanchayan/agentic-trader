## Which Setups Are Armed

On a MIXED/RANGE day, the priority order is:

- **ORB Breakout (primary):** Valid for AMZN, AAPL, NFLX, NVDA (conditional). Requires:
  price breaks above the first-15-minute high, spread <15 bp at evaluation time, relative
  volume >1.5x, entry no later than 11:00 AM ET (90-minute ORB window), and a confirmed
  companion stop order placed with the broker on the same tick as the BUY.
- **VWAP Reclaim (alternate for NFLX):** If NFLX does not produce a clean ORB break but
  holds above VWAP with rising volume through 10:15 AM, a VWAP reclaim long is valid as
  a secondary approach. Require rel_vol >1.5x and spread <15 bp.
- **Momentum:** DO NOT use. Momentum setup record is 0/1 wins (-0.975R). The additional
  confirmations required (sector alignment, entry within 60 minutes of the ORB) are hard to
  meet on a range day. Skip momentum entirely today.
- **Mean-reversion:** Do not use today. On a range day it is theoretically available, but
  none of our names have a mean-reversion thesis today, and the INTC offering creates a
  genuine directional overhang that makes fading dips in semis dangerous.

**Target sizing (apply to all ORB entries today):** Set target at 1.5x to 2.0x per-share
risk above entry. Do not use 2.5x ATR as the target — on a mixed/range tape, that translates
to 5-6R and is structurally unreachable in a single session. The lesson from 2026-08-07 is
clear: a $79.12 target on a $2.08-ATR stock captured only 0.345R before EOD flatten.

**2:1 R/R minimum:** Before submitting any order, verify that the distance from entry to
target is at least 2x the distance from entry to stop. Skip any setup where this math does
not work at the actual entry price.

**Companion stop:** Every BUY order must be accompanied by a companion stop order placed
with the broker on the same tick. The EOD flatten is a safety net, not a stop loss. Do not
enter a position without a live broker stop in place (lesson from 2026-07-31: AMZN fell
$4.34/share past the defined stop before EOD flatten ran).
