## What Worked / What Did Not

**What worked:**

- **A premarket plan was written before the open.** `docs/plan/2026-11-02.md` was written at
  ~9:28 AM ET. The plan correctly identified ISM at 10:00 AM as compressing the ORB window,
  midterm election uncertainty as background caution, and NFLX as the primary candidate with
  the cleanest premarket spread. The plan's analysis was accurate — NFLX's spread did tighten
  to 1.6 bp and it was above VWAP at mid-session.

- **PRECONDITION #1 was enforced correctly.** The plan's own conditional language stated that
  the halt remained active if the scheduler was not confirmed running at the first tick. The tick
  loop applied this rule at every tick without loosening it. The plan was written but the
  condition it set was not met. The system did not take a shortcut.

- **The broad-tape unlock gate held independently.** Even setting PRECONDITION #1 aside, the
  broad-tape unlock failed at 11:55 AM (SPY and NVDA both below VWAP). Two independent reasons
  to not trade existed simultaneously. The system identified both.

- **Capital preserved at $999,641.65.** No loss. Account is flat.

**What did not work:**

- **PRECONDITION #1 has been the blocking condition for 36+ consecutive sessions.** The
  CronList is empty. The 3:50 / 3:53 / 3:56 PM ET flatten passes have not been independently
  confirmed running since September 17. October 30's lesson explicitly listed "fix the CronList
  before the Monday open" as the first priority. It was not fixed. Today's journal repeats the
  same finding for at least the fourth time.

- **The conditional acknowledgment mechanism is not sufficient for unattended sessions.** The
  plan includes a human-signed acknowledgment that addresses PRECONDITION #1, but the
  acknowledgment is explicitly voided if the scheduler is not confirmed at the first tick. In
  practice, the scheduler has never been confirmed. The acknowledgment exists in the plan but
  never clears the halt in the tick loop. This means the bot cannot trade at all until the
  scheduler is actually running.

- **NFLX had a usable setup that could not be acted on.** At 11:55 AM, NFLX was above VWAP
  with a 1.6 bp spread. If the flatten scheduler had been running and the broad-tape unlock had
  been in effect, this might have been a tradeable VWAP reclaim Tick 1. Instead, it was observed
  and logged and ignored. The bot cannot extract value from correct analysis when infrastructure
  blockers prevent execution.

- **The all-time Sharpe ratio of -93.4 is a direct consequence of this pattern.** Six winning
  trades across 94 days, with a win rate of 19.35%, cannot overcome even minor early losses
  when trading has been effectively frozen for months. The metric will not improve without
  executed roundtrips.
