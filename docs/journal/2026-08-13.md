## Lessons for Tomorrow

1. **Investigate the premarket-to-first-tick timing gap.** At 9:40 AM, the tick loop found
   `docs/plan/2026-08-13.md` absent even though the premarket phase had run. Either the plan
   file is written late in the premarket sequence, or the first tick fires too soon. A simple
   fix: in the safety gate (`code/preflight.py`), treat a missing plan file as HALT but also
   log the specific missing path so the next postmarket review can identify whether this is a
   write-time issue or a file-naming mismatch.

2. **A MIXED regime day with only one armed candidate and no strong volume catalyst is
   correctly a zero-trade day.** NVDA never produced 1.5x relative volume, never broke the
   ORB high, and the tape did not trend. Every disciplined parameter said "wait." Waiting was
   right. The lesson is not "we missed a trade" — it is "the plan was accurate and the system
   behaved correctly."

3. **The 3-session micro-losing-streak (Aug 11 flat, Aug 12 -$47.74, Aug 13 flat) keeps the
   tuner frozen, which is correct.** When there is no positive evidence from recent sessions,
   do not adjust settings. The freeze protects us from tightening gates in response to one
   loss and then missing a legitimate trade next week. Let the tuner unfreeze on its own
   evidence-based logic.
