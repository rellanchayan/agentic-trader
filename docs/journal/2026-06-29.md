   that the tick loop is actually running at 2-minute intervals during market hours and that its

   failed to produce it. The journal cannot honestly evaluate whether the bot is doing its job without
   knowing what the tick loop saw and decided. If the intraday log does not exist tomorrow, the cause
   must be diagnosed before the session after.

   ever (NVDA +1.36R, MSFT -2.80R, AMZN -0.975R), there is not enough data to improve any parameter.
