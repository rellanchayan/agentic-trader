## Setups Armed

**Preferred setup on MIXED/RANGE tape: VWAP reclaim.**

In a range/non-trending market, buying a VWAP reclaim (price dips, finds support, recovers
above VWAP with volume) gives a defined stop (below the session low) and a realistic target
(prior resistance or a 1.5–2.0x R/R level that is reachable in the session).

**Secondary setup: ORB breakout — only with stronger confirmation.**

Because this is not a risk-on trending day, an ORB breakout requires the same 1.5x rel vol
minimum but also confirmation that the broader tape (SPY, QQQ) is not breaking down. If SPY
is below its own ORB low while we are considering an ORB buy in INTC, that is swimming
against the tide. Both the individual name and the broader market must be constructive.

**Setup sizing on a range day:**

Because the tape is not trending, use a 1.5–2.0x R/R target rather than the default 2.5x
ATR target. On a quiet range day a 2.5x ATR target may never be reached, and the flatten
at 3:55 PM will cut the winner short. Set a tighter, achievable target.
