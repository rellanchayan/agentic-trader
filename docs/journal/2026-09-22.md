## The Carryover Close

**NVDA — Forced Liquidation of September 21 Overnight Carryover**

The position: 44 NVDA shares, originally entered September 21 at 10:49 AM ET as an ORB
breakout at $224.46 average fill. The flatten attempt on September 21 fired at 4:00:41 PM ET —
41 seconds after the market close — and was immediately canceled by Alpaca. The position was
carried overnight involuntarily.

At 9:35 AM ET on September 22, a LIMIT SELL for 44 NVDA at $228.71 was submitted. The order
filled at **$229.16** average (better than the limit). The position was fully closed.

Cross-day P&L: 44 × ($229.16 – $224.46) = **+$206.80 gross**.

This is not a trading success. NVDA happened to open higher, so the carryover resolved with a
gain. If NVDA had gapped down overnight — an entirely plausible outcome for a single-name
semiconductor stock — the loss would have been uncontrolled and the flatten-rule violation would
have cost real money in addition to the rule violation. The fact that we profited from the
violation makes it more dangerous, not less: it creates a false signal that carrying overnight
is acceptable.

The position is now closed. The violation is recorded.

## What Worked / What Did Not Work

**What worked:**

- The NVDA carryover was liquidated at the open cleanly. Fill was $229.16 — above the $228.71
  limit. Executed within 5 minutes of the open.
- No new positions were opened without a day plan or flatten confirmation. The system correctly
  did nothing rather than compound the problem.
- Loss-stop never approached. Day-stop was reset at open per normal procedure.
- Fill rate was 100% for today's single order.

**What did not work:**

- The flatten scheduler is still broken. Today's carryover close was a consequence of that bug.
  The bug was first identified September 17. It is now September 22. Five trading days have
  passed with no fix in place.
- No premarket run means no day plan. A session without a plan is a session without the
  foundation for any legitimate entry. The premarket routine should run every trading day,
  independent of whether new positions will be opened.

## Lessons for Tomorrow (2026-09-23)

1. **The flatten scheduler must be fixed before September 23 opens.** The idempotency logic must
   treat CANCELED orders as requiring a fresh submission. The 3:50 PM, 3:53 PM, and 3:56 PM
   passes must fire on schedule. This is a binary requirement: fixed or no new entries.

2. **The premarket routine must run regardless of carryover status.** A day that begins with a
   forced liquidation still needs a plan for the remainder of the session. No plan = no entries,
   but the lack of plan is itself correctable by running the premarket screen even if late.

3. **Carryover gains are not trading skill.** NVDA moved in our favor overnight and we booked
   $206.80. None of that reflects a decision made on September 22. It reflects overnight luck.
   Do not let a profitable resolution of a rule violation create any comfort with rule violations.
