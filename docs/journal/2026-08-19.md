## Lessons for Tomorrow

1. **Gap-up MIXED/RANGE mornings produce ORB breakout tape, not VWAP reclaim tape.** When
   SPY opens above VWAP on positive catalysts and holds, the reclaim setup never appears.
   On future MIXED/RANGE gap-up days, weight the ORB breakout setup as primary and the VWAP
   reclaim as secondary (or drop it entirely), and document that weighting in the plan. Do
   not list VWAP reclaim as the "preferred" setup on a tape that structurally cannot deliver
   the required flush.

2. **1.72x is not 2.0x, and the gate deserved to hold — but note the regime mismatch.** The
   ORB breakout was real (SPY confirmed above $770.59 ORB high at $771.11). The volume
   shortfall was 0.28x. On a cleaner day with no 2:00 PM event wall, the plan should note
   whether that borderline case warrants a closer look. For today the gate was right. Tomorrow
   the question is: is 2.0x the correct gate for all MIXED/RANGE gap-up days, or should the
   threshold be tiered (e.g., 1.8x when spread is below 2 bp and the 2:1 R/R is confirmed)?

3. **Eight consecutive non-profitable sessions deserve a systematic look, not a forced trade.**
   The correct response to a streak is to check the playbook rules, not to lower the gates.
   Before the next session, review whether the premarket screen is generating enough
   qualifying setups per day, whether the ORB volume gate is set correctly for current
   market conditions, and whether there are additional liquid names (e.g., IWM) that could
   supplement SPY and QQQ in low-vol regimes. If the rules are correct and the market is not
   providing setups, staying flat is the right trade.
