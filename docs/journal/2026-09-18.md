## Lessons for Tomorrow

1. **BAC needs a live-data audit before it is listed as a candidate again.** Three consecutive
   ticks with no live data is not bad luck — it is a data pipeline problem. Either the context
   snapshot for BAC is broken, or BAC requires a different data source. Diagnose before the
   next session where BAC appears in the plan.

2. **Catalyst stacking does not overcome a broken tape.** INTC had analyst upgrades from Citi,
   UBS, and Northland, plus a CPU price hike and a fab-collaboration story — and still fell $3.60
   from its premarket high while spending the entire session below VWAP. The catalog of catalysts
   is research input, not a trade trigger. Price and volume are the only real triggers.

3. **The flatten system must be actively verified before each session, not assumed working.** The
   September 17 failure still echoes two days later (in the form of a -$30.46 cross-day loss).
   Before each open, run a quick status check that the flatten scheduler is scheduled and reachable.
   If it cannot be confirmed, do not open new positions that day.
