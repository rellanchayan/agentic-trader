## Lessons for Tomorrow (Wednesday July 8)

1. Premarket must fire before 9:30 AM ET, every day the market is open — no exceptions. Without
   the premarket phase, the bot has no basis for any decision and the entire session is wasted.
   Verify that state/day_plan.json was written before ticks begin.

2. A missing plan should trigger an explicit logged alert, not silent inaction. The system
   should detect the absence of today's day_plan.json at the start of the first tick and write
   an entry to the intraday log explaining that it is standing aside due to missing premarket
   context. Silent skipping of a full session is harder to diagnose than a logged abort.

3. Wednesday July 8 carries FOMC minutes risk at 2:00 PM ET (flagged in the July 3 plan).
   The premarket plan for tomorrow must note this, narrow the trading window to the morning
   session (9:30 AM – 1:30 PM ET), and treat 2:00 PM as a hard stop for new entries.
