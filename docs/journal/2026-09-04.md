   cautious override to second-guess.** NVDA peaked at 1.70x on a $12.93B acquisition day during
   an NFP session and still failed to hold volume. The 2.0x threshold correctly predicted that the
   tape did not have the institutional conviction needed for an ORB entry. Next week: enforce
   the elevated threshold without hesitation on any day tagged MIXED/RANGE or with an imminent
   macro event.

2. **A disarmed name can show a technical signal. The disarm wins.** INTC's orb_breakout=true at
   14:40 is the clearest example this program has seen of a technical signal appearing on a name
   that was explicitly excluded for session-level structural reasons (spread-blowout history, no
   catalyst). The disarm policy is correct and complete — it does not require re-evaluation when a
   signal appears. Update the playbook to state this explicitly: "If a name is disarmed for the
   session, any technical signal during that session is recorded in the log and ignored."

3. **The vwap_reclaim setup has not won in two live appearances, both in MIXED/RANGE conditions.
   Before the next vwap_reclaim entry, the playbook should require a TREND regime tag, not just
   elevated relative volume.** The plan already says VWAP reclaim requires a TREND tape to be
   armed. Reinforce that this is a hard gate, not a soft preference — MIXED/RANGE disarms the
   entire setup, regardless of how clean the price action looks in isolation.
