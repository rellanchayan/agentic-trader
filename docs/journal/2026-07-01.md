## Lessons for Tomorrow

1. An empty day_plan.md and an empty intraday_log.md on the same day is not a coincidence of
   no setups — it is a confirmed infrastructure failure. The scheduler or the phase scripts are
   not executing reliably. Before the next session, verify that the premarket phase actually runs
   and writes to docs/day_plan.md, and that the tick loop produces at least one log entry within
   the first 10 minutes after open. If those two checks fail, the bot is not trading — it is
   silently doing nothing.

2. Four consecutive no-trade sessions is a pattern that must be explained, not accepted. Two
   of those sessions (2026-06-26, 2026-06-29) were noted as suspicious at the time. Today's
   session has zero output from any phase. The streak is directionally wrong: a functioning
   intraday bot with 6.5 hours of market time per day should find at least some setups across
   four sessions. Either the market has been unusually quiet (possible, but not confirmed) or the
   bot is not looking. The evidence points to the latter.

3. The existing trade statistics are all negative in expectancy. When the infrastructure is
   repaired and trading resumes, the first priority is not to generate more trades — it is to
   generate correct trades. The playbook's ORB and momentum setups are both showing negative R
   expectancy. Review entry criteria before the next live session, not after.
