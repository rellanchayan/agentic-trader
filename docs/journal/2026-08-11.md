confirms zero trades but does not expose which names were evaluated at which ticks, what
the live spread readings were, or what rel_vol was at each evaluation. Future journals

describe the planning logic, not the execution logic.

**Four of the seven names on the watchlist were disarmed before the open.** On a session
where only three names are armed going into the tape, and two of those three require
additional conditional checks (NVDA on sector, QQQ on both NVDA and NFLX failure), the
watchlist was thin. A pre-CPI Tuesday is not the session to build out the list — but the
structural reality is that most of the liquid large-cap universe was either in blowout
spread territory (AMZN at 328.1 bp, AAPL at 33.7 bp), structurally disarmed (INTC), or
unviable on R/R (PFE). This is the tape, not a planning failure. The correct response
is to document it and accept zero trades.

**The AMZN 328.1 bp premarket spread adds a fourth data point to a persistent pattern.**
Prior sessions: 2026-08-05 (premarket ~5 bp, live opening spread 153 bp), 2026-08-07
(premarket 4.1 bp, live 28.8 bp), 2026-08-10 (premarket 4.4 bp, live spread unknown but

on at least two consecutive sessions without a gap or news catalyst.
