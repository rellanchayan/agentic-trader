## Lessons for Tomorrow

1. Verify market hours directly from Alpaca before writing the day plan's operational rules. The
   plan stated a 1:00 PM ET close with high confidence; Alpaca showed normal hours. The PLAYBOOK
   should require a clock check as the first step in premarket writing, and any early-close rules
   should be explicitly sourced to Alpaca's calendar, not assumed from the date.

2. The INTC premarket bounce failed exactly as the plan's risk section warned it might — Korean
   chip sector pressure overwhelmed the bounce thesis. When a stock's most important risk factor
   has already materialized overnight (Kospi -7.9% at the time of plan writing), the bounce thesis
   deserves less confidence, not more. Consider dropping gap-bounce setups on semiconductor names
   when sector-wide selling is confirmed premarket.

3. The tick loop must produce a log entry for every decision cycle, not just certain ones. One
   entry across a full session is not an adequate audit trail. Before the next session, confirm
   that the tick loop is writing to docs/intraday/<date>.md on every pass, even when the decision
   is "no action." Silent ticks cannot be reviewed, cannot be improved, and cannot be trusted.
