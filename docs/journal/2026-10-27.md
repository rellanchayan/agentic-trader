## What Worked / What Did Not

**What worked:**

- **Premarket ran and wrote a plan.** After 31 consecutive sessions where no plan was written
  (except the isolated October 23 anomaly), the premarket phase produced `docs/day_plan.md`
  today. This is the single most important operational improvement since September 17.

- **The plan was correctly calibrated for FOMC Day 1.** Using an elevated relative-volume gate
  (2.0x), disarming two of three setups, adding a broad-tape unlock condition, and cutting the
  entry window to 1:30 PM ET is exactly the right level of caution on the day before a Fed
  decision.

- **A zero-trade outcome was pre-acknowledged.** The plan explicitly stated zero trades was the
  likely and correct result if gates did not pass. When the outcome matched the plan, that is
  not a failure — it is the system working as designed.

- **Capital is intact at $999,641.65.** No unauthorized trades. No loss-stop triggered. The
  flatten phase confirmed flat at close.

- **Max drawdown remains -0.03%.** Against SPY's -3.36%, the account has preserved nearly all
  starting capital over 88 trading days. In a period of zero trading, preservation is the only
  metric with any meaning.

**What did not work:**

- **The tick loop did not log any entries.** The intraday log shows 0 entries. The plan was
  written, but there is no record of tick evaluations running between 9:30 AM and 3:58 PM. If
  ticks had run, they would have produced at least a log line on each pass — even a HOLD or
  GATE_BLOCKED entry. Zero log entries means zero ticks were invoked. PRECONDITION #1
  (CronList / scheduled tick loop) remains unresolved.

- **No real-time tape observation occurred.** Even though no trades were expected, running ticks
  would have produced regime and tape observations that inform tomorrow's setup. With zero tick
  entries, today generated zero learning data beyond what the premarket wrote.

- **The Sharpe ratio continues to degrade.** -91.34 today. This number is not useful at 32
  no-trade sessions but is recorded honestly. Each zero-variance day adds roughly 0.5 points
  of degradation.

## Lessons for Tomorrow (2026-10-28, Wednesday — FOMC Rate Decision Day)

1. **The premarket is back but the tick loop is still broken.** A plan without active ticks is
   a plan that cannot execute. Even if tomorrow's setups arm and the broad tape clears, zero

2. **Tomorrow is FOMC Rate Decision day — the hardest day of the month to day-trade.** The
   2:00 PM ET announcement creates a binary event that compresses spreads beforehand and
   creates extreme whipsaw volatility afterward. The correct posture is likely no entries before
   2:00 PM (FOMC pre-announcement compression) and extreme caution after. If the plan for
   tomorrow does not explicitly address FOMC reaction behavior, do not trade the post-announcement
   period without one.

3. **Today proved the re-calibration posture works.** No capital was lost. The plan matched
   reality. The hardest discipline on a re-entry day is not forcing a trade to prove the system
   is alive. Today passed that test. Carry the same discipline into FOMC day: it is better to
   watch and learn than to deploy into a Fed announcement.
