## Lessons for Tomorrow

1. The premarket plan worked today. That habit must be maintained. A plan written before the open
   is not extra work — it is the difference between a day we can learn from and a day that is
   invisible. Write it every session, even if the conclusion is "no setups qualify today."

2. The intraday log was not written today, leaving a gap in the audit trail. Even on a no-trade day,
   the tick loop should write a brief log entry at each evaluation: what was scanned, what was
   blocked and why, what time the session ended. Without that log, a no-trade day cannot be
   distinguished from a day when the scanner failed to run at all.

3. Seven sessions in, we have completed only two round-trips total (NVDA +1.36R, MSFT -2.80R,
   AMZN -0.975R, BAC approximately -1R or worse). The sample is too small for any statistical
   conclusion, but the direction is clear: all four trades are losses except NVDA, and the MSFT
   loss was nearly 3R. Before the next trade, confirm that the stop placement logic is sizing the
   stop correctly — a -2.80R loss on a trade means the stop was either not placed where it should
   have been, or was placed correctly but a fast move caused significant slippage. Review the MSFT
   trade specifically.
