### Post-Trade Note: 0.355R vs 1.75R

The 0.355R outcome reflects a structural constraint, not a failed trade. A 1.75R target on
NVDA from a $207.22 entry requires a $16.72/share move — approximately 8% from entry. With
entry at 70 minutes into the session and an EOD flatten required by ~3:55 PM ET, the trade
had approximately 5 hours to develop that move. It did not. Whether 5 hours was ever
realistically sufficient for an 8% intraday move in a MIXED / RANGE regime is worth honest
evaluation: on days where the plan labels the regime as range-bound, a 1.75R ORB target
assumes a breakout strong enough to trend for most of the session. That assumption needs to
be tested against the actual distribution of NVDA ORB moves on mixed-regime days. For now,
the honest record is: 0.355R captured, 1.415R left on the table due to the flatten rule, no
rules broken.
