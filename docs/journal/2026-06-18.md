  stop can distort the R calculation when the exit is taken at a clean limit well below that stop.

## Lessons for Tomorrow

1. A -2.803R loss from a setup that promised -1R means the stop was too tight for the instrument's
   noise at that time of day. Before entering, compare the stop distance to the stock's average
   intraday range: if the stop is less than one average true range move, it will get tagged by noise
   before price has a chance to prove the thesis wrong.

2. ORB setups have a time window. The opening range forms in the first 30 minutes. An "ORB breakout"
   entered 4 hours into the session (1:36 PM) is not the same trade as one entered at 10:15 AM.
   Respect the time-of-day filter and do not apply an ORB label to a breakout that happens to be
   above some morning high — check whether the original range still has structural relevance.

3. For the third consecutive session, there was no premarket run and no written plan. This is now
   a pattern, not a one-off. Without a baseline equity snapshot at the open, the $3,000 loss-stop
   has no anchor. Fix the premarket automation before trading again.
